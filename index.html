The Eye

2026 · GLSL / WebGL · Real-time

A living iris enclosing an entire galaxy within its pupil. Concentric ring-segments rotate at individually hashed rates, their pigmentation drifting through a slow cosine palette. At the centre, a Milky Way band tilted 35° across the dark pupil holds nebula haze, a warm galactic core, and four layers of twinkling stars — the universe glimpsed through a single watching eye.

Be Not Afraid

                        
2026 · GLSL / WebGL · Real-time

                        

                        
An eye as wide as the sky — its iris a carousel of slowly rotating ring-segments, its gaze drifting in long unhurried saccades across the scene. Five-pointed rays of gold radiate from the centre while a pale pupil holds a single dark point of stillness. Far below in the lower reaches, a small Earth hangs — blue oceans bleeding into green continents — watched over without blinking.
