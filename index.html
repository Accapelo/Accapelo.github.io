The Eye

2026 · GLSL / WebGL · Real-time

A living iris enclosing an entire galaxy within its pupil. Concentric ring-segments rotate at individually hashed rates, their pigmentation drifting through a slow cosine palette. At the centre, a Milky Way band tilted 35° across the dark pupil holds nebula haze, a warm galactic core, and four layers of twinkling stars — the universe glimpsed through a single watching eye.

Be Not Afraid

                        
2026 · GLSL / WebGL · Real-time

                        

                        
An eye as wide as the sky — its iris a carousel of slowly rotating ring-segments, its gaze drifting in long unhurried saccades across the scene. Five-pointed rays of gold radiate from the centre while a pale pupil holds a single dark point of stillness. Far below in the lower reaches, a small Earth hangs — blue oceans bleeding into green continents — watched over without blinking.

All-Seeing Eye

                        
2026 · GLSL / WebGL · Real-time

                        

                        
An eye that sees everything — and cannot look away. Colour fractures through it like light through a cathedral window, planes folding and refolding along edges that glow where they meet. The pupil roves in slow deliberate saccades, and wherever it settles the surrounding field bends inward toward it, as though sight itself distorts the world it falls upon.

Multi-camera photogrammetry scan

                        
2019 · Three.js / Blender / Google Face landmark detection · Interactive

                        

                        
While I was doing an internship in Shanghai during my last year of university the company had a multi-camera photogrammetry rig with 100+ cameras that they allowed me and another friend to scan our faces in (since we were shaved and hair does not work well with this technique). They mostly wanted assets for potential game characters in the future but were nice enough to give us the files as well for free use. While the original 3D model display every pore and imperfection on my face in crazy detail it is also above 1GB+ in size which makes it not too great for web viewing. I've managed to compress in to <10mb while still maintaining most of its details and structure to display it here. It's also pretty fun to have your 23 year old self immortalized like this in computer form.

                        
                        
                        
* Since I am not a professional animator the face tracking animations of my face scan are very bare-boned.
